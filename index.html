A legacy of authentic South Indian flavours

Simple Food. Timeless Tradition.

Since 1965, Brahmins' Coffee Bar has been part of Bengaluru's breakfast culture. Known for its traditional South Indian vegetarian breakfast, this iconic establishment celebrates the beauty of simple food prepared and served with consistency.

From soft, comforting idlis to golden crispy vadas and aromatic filter coffee, every offering reflects the charm of traditional Bengaluru breakfast culture.

A focused menu, familiar flavours, and a fast-paced local café atmosphere make it a distinctive breakfast destination.

Idli

BREAKFAST

Vada

Golden, crispy South Indian savoury fritters with a deliciously soft interior.

SAVOURY

Khara Bath

A comforting savoury semolina preparation, delicately seasoned with traditional spices.

SWEET

Kesari Bath

A rich, sweet semolina delicacy prepared with ghee, cashews, and raisins.

Chow Chow Bath

A delightful combination of savoury khara bath and sweet kesari bath.
